2. Data We Collect

StandShift is designed with privacy by default. We collect and process only the minimum data required to provide sit-stand timer reminders, posture tracking, and wellness statistics across iOS, macOS, and watchOS:

  • Posture & Session Data: Standing and sitting session durations, timestamps, selected work modes, and daily/weekly goal progress stored locally on your device via Core Data.
  • Health Data (Optional): If you explicitly grant Apple HealthKit permission, StandShift reads and writes relevant activity metrics (standing time, step count, active energy, and workouts). HealthKit data never leaves your personal devices or Apple's encrypted Health store and is never used for advertising or shared with third parties.
  • Calendar & Context Data (Optional): If you grant Calendar or Location permissions for Smart Features, StandShift checks event busy windows and workplace geofences purely on-device to pause or adapt reminders during meetings.
  • Synchronization Data (Optional): When iCloud Sync is enabled, your preferences and session records are synchronized across your own Apple devices using Apple CloudKit (container iCloud.com.ydodevelopment.standshift) and iCloud Key-Value Storage inside your private iCloud account.
  • Technical & Diagnostic Data (Optional): Local performance metrics and crash diagnostics if you opt into analytics consent in the app's Privacy settings.

StandShift does not require account registration, does not collect your name or address, and does not track you across third-party apps or websites.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

  • Explicit Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) & Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR): Processing of optional HealthKit metrics, iCloud synchronization, notifications, and analytics, which you can toggle at any time under Settings → Privacy & Data.
  • Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): Providing core sit-stand timer functionality, statistics, and subscription features you request.
  • Legitimate Interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): Maintaining app stability, security, and local error recovery.

4. Data Sharing and International Transfers

  • No Third-Party Selling or Advertising: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data to any third party, data broker, or advertiser.
  • Service Providers: Multi-device synchronization and in-app purchases are handled exclusively by Apple Inc. (iCloud / CloudKit / StoreKit) under Apple's applicable terms and Standard Contractual Clauses where international transfers occur.

5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

  • On-Device & Private iCloud Storage: Data is stored locally on your Apple devices and, if enabled, in your private iCloud database encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Retention & Auto-Deletion: Session data is retained on your device until you delete the app or trigger data deletion. You may also enable automatic deletion after 90 days directly in Settings → Privacy & Data.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR & CCPA

You have full, self-service control over your data directly inside StandShift (Settings → Privacy & Data):

  • Right of Access & Data Portability: Use Export All Data to export a complete machine-readable JSON copy of your records at any time.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Use Delete All Data to permanently erase all local and synchronized records.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Toggle Analytics, HealthKit Sync, iCloud Sync, or Notifications off at any time.

To exercise any privacy right or ask questions, contact us at privacy@standshift.app.
